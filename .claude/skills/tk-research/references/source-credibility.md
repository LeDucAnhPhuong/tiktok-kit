# Source Credibility

How to weigh and label what research finds.

## Tiers

| Tier | Source | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Platform's own documentation, help center, developer docs | highest — the only tier that settles what is actually available |
| 2 | Platform announcements, official blog, press release | high for intent, **weak for availability** |
| 3 | On-platform observed data — real videos, real engagement | high for what is working now |
| 4 | Established industry press with named reporting | medium |
| 5 | Vendor blogs, agency posts, SEO content marketing | low — usually restating tier 2 with added confidence |
| 6 | Social posts, forum comments, anecdote | lowest — signal for what to check, never evidence |

Three tier-5 pages restating the same tier-2 announcement count as **one** source, not
three. Independence means independent observation, not independent URLs.

## Labels

Every claim carries one:

| Label | Criteria |
|---|---|
| `official` | tier 1, or tier 2 read directly from the platform |
| `reported` | tier 4 or 5 describing a platform statement |
| `speculative` | analysis, prediction, or inference by anyone |

The common failure this prevents: a tier-5 post says "TikTok now lets you X", and X is
in fact announced but unavailable. Reading tier 1 settles it.

## Announced Versus Available

Check both. A feature can be announced with a launch date, covered widely, and still
have no public endpoint, no documentation, and no access path.

State the status explicitly:

- **available** — documented, with an access path
- **announced** — stated by the platform, no access path published
- **rumored** — reported without platform confirmation

## Staleness

| Subject | Window | Past it |
|---|---|---|
| Trends, sounds, hashtags | 30 days | possibly decayed — verify before acting |
| Formats, audience behavior | 90 days | dated — direction may hold, specifics may not |
| Platform mechanics, ad products | 180 days | possibly superseded — re-check tier 1 |

Windows are **provisional** — estimates, not measurements. Same status as the
diagnostic thresholds. Calibrate against observed decay and record the change.

## Minimum Standard

A market claim ships only with: three independent sources, a date on each, a tier for
each, a label, and a URL.

Below that standard, report it as a lead worth checking rather than as a finding.
