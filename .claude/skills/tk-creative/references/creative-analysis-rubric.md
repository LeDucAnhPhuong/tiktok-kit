# Creative Analysis Rubric

What to look at, in order. Applies to the actual media — this rubric cannot be
completed from metrics or captions alone.

## Minimum Sample

Below `min_videos_for_pattern` (default 8) videos with meaningful delivery, this skill
produces **hypotheses**, not findings. Say so in the output. A pattern drawn from three
videos is a coincidence with good presentation.

Also required: winners and losers must actually differ on the trait. A trait present
in every video explains nothing, however strongly it correlates with success.

## 1. The Opening

TikTok's completion economics are decided in the first seconds.

| Check | What to note |
|---|---|
| First frame | Is there a subject, or does it open on a title card? |
| First 1s | Movement, face, or text? Static openings lose |
| First 3s | Is the promise stated — what will the viewer get? |
| Hook type | question, claim, demonstration, pattern-break, before/after |
| Audio start | speech, music, silence |

## 2. Pacing

| Check | What to note |
|---|---|
| Cut frequency | seconds per cut, and whether it varies |
| Dead air | any stretch with no visual or verbal change |
| Payoff timing | where the promised result lands relative to duration |
| Ending | hard stop, loop, or call to action |

## 3. On-Screen Text

| Check | What to note |
|---|---|
| Presence | first text appearance time |
| Legibility | size relative to frame, contrast against background |
| Safe area | is text hidden behind the UI overlay at bottom and right |
| Redundancy | does text repeat the audio or add to it |

## 4. Framing And Composition

| Check | What to note |
|---|---|
| Aspect | is it native vertical, or repurposed with letterboxing |
| Subject placement | centered and clear of UI, or drifting behind it |
| Visual density | readable on a phone at arm's length |
| Lighting | subject distinguishable from background |

## 5. Thumbnail

| Check | What to note |
|---|---|
| Selected frame | deliberate or default |
| Face | present and legible at small size |
| Text | readable at profile-grid size |

## 6. Audio

| Check | What to note |
|---|---|
| Track | trending sound, original audio, or none |
| Speech clarity | intelligible without captions |
| Music vs voice balance | does music mask speech |

## 7. Structure

| Check | What to note |
|---|---|
| Format | talking head, demonstration, listicle, story, skit, UGC-style |
| Length | absolute, and versus the account's own median |
| Single idea | one idea per video, or several competing |

## Confound Checklist

Before attributing anything to creative, rule out:

- **Spend** — a paid-boosted video outperforms on delivery, not merit
- **Placement** — feed versus other surfaces changes attention baseline
- **Duration** — completion rate falls with length; compare like with like
- **Posting time and day**
- **Topic novelty** — first video on a topic often beats later ones regardless of craft
- **Follower growth during the window** — a larger audience lifts everything

Anything not ruled out goes in the confounded bucket. Not the winners bucket.

## Output Discipline

Each pattern states: the trait, how many winners have it, how many losers have it,
what could not be ruled out, and the concrete test that would confirm or kill it.

A pattern with no falsifying test is an opinion.
